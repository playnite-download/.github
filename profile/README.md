# 🎮 Playnite – Open-Source Game Library & Launcher for Windows

📌 **About Playnite (Windows)**  
**Playnite** is an **open-source game launcher and library manager** designed to bring all your games — from **Steam, GOG, Epic Games, Origin, Battle.net**, and more — into one clean and customizable interface.  

It supports **metadata scraping, themes, extensions, and full controller navigation**, offering a unified gaming hub that combines convenience with freedom. Playnite is perfect for players who want full control over their digital library.

## 🎯 Key Features
🎮 **Unified Library** – Combine all games from multiple launchers in one place.  
🧩 **Plugin & Extension Support** – Expand functionality through community plugins.  
🎨 **Theme Customization** – Modify layout, colors, and interface visuals.  
⚙️ **Metadata Scraping** – Automatically fetch game covers, info, and ratings.  
🕹️ **Fullscreen Mode** – Console-like navigation for controller users.  
📊 **Playtime Tracking** – Monitor play hours and session history.  
🌍 **Cross-Launcher Sync** – Integrate with Steam, Epic, GOG, Origin, and more.  
🚀 **Fast Performance** – Lightweight and efficient for large libraries.  
💡 **Open Source** – Free to use and community-maintained.  
🔒 **Privacy Respecting** – No telemetry or user tracking.  
📥 **Auto Update System** – Keep Playnite and plugins up to date automatically.  
🎯 **Game Launch Customization** – Configure pre-launch and post-launch scripts.  
🎮 **Emulator Support** – Manage and launch retro games easily.  
📈 **Activity Logs** – View gaming statistics and achievements.  
💻 **Windows Optimized** – Runs seamlessly on Windows 10/11 systems.
